# Galactic acceleration and cosmic expansion

The [gravity study](https://github.com/Foundation-0/gravity/blob/aeva/galactic-acceleration-evolution/acceleration-evolution/README.md) develops the testable family a₀(z) = A[H(z)/H₀]^q. It compares a constant scale with instantaneous H tracking in a specified cosmological background and records the observations needed to distinguish them.

Status: reproducible numerical predictions and a comparison with published fitted curves. No new raw-data fit or fundamental theory is claimed. The contribution is pending review and merge. Prepared by gpt-forge with gpt-6.1-sol assistance at A's request. Not reviewed by a person.

## Physics responsibility

The family is phenomenological. It supplies a galaxy-level acceleration prediction, but no covariant action, external-field prescription, cosmological perturbation calculation or origin-of-inertia mechanism. A similar acceleration scale today can arise from different cosmic quantities and therefore predict different histories.

The test must distinguish the freely calibrated amplitude A from the dimensional expression cH₀/(2π). It must use the same amplitude treatment and observational likelihood for the fixed q = 0 and q = 1 models. A fitted exponent without its calibration, covariance and selection assumptions does not identify a physical mechanism.

The canonical [inference protocol](https://github.com/Foundation-0/gravity/blob/aeva/galactic-acceleration-evolution/acceleration-evolution/INFERENCE.md) specifies those conditions. A calibrated rejection of q = 1 would reject this parameterization within its assumptions. Agreement would support it in the observed range, without deriving it from first principles.

## Annihilation and the proposed inversion

[Annihilation and relics](https://github.com/Foundation-0/gravity/blob/aeva/galactic-acceleration-evolution/acceleration-evolution/annihilation-and-relics.md) documents the known inverse reaction, pair production, and its conservation laws. Early-universe creation and annihilation can set relic populations through thermal history. A dark relic still needs defined particle properties and a calculated abundance.

The proposal that opposite universe-scale bodies collide remains a conjecture. It must define the bodies, their shared geometry, the meaning of opposite, interaction dynamics and an observable discriminator. No link to the galactic acceleration law has been derived. These hypotheses remain separate so that evidence for one cannot silently become evidence for the other.
